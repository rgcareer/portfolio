## 2026-09-15 — home/sitewide: "warm & premium, alive" color evolution (COMMITTED strategy, feel-gate PASS)
- **What happened:** after the Slice 0+1 home rebuild, Ryan flagged three-plus times that the page still read as a static/plain site ("not super happy with the color and lack of life"). foundry diagnosis (BRAND register): the home was colored with a RESTRAINED strategy (one accent held under ~10% on warm white) — the product/minimalism FLOOR, wrong for a brand surface. Fix = a COMMITTED color strategy (foundry `color.md`), not more motion.
- **Chosen (approved at the 2026-09-15 feel-gate, "so far the direction looks good, let's continue on"):** (1) the sanctioned apricot ELEVATED to a real graphic color — `--amber #E8A54D` / `--amber-soft #F6C98A` — for figures/rules/glows ON DARK GROUNDS only (never small light text on white; fails AA there). (2) burgundy DRENCHES a whole band: the `.proof` section inverts to a deep `--burgundy` ground, `--on-deep #FBEDE4` text (9.66:1), glowing amber figures (5.23:1) — the color centerpiece. (3) section color rhythm via `--blush #F7F0EC` (`.sec-blush`): warm-white → blush → wine-band alternation, not one flat sheet. (4) the WebGL daylight field enlarged (`min(660px,68vw)`) and made present in the hero. (5) bolder numerals as anchors; `--deep-line #8E3B48` for rules on the band.
- **Why this, not the alternatives (Ryan picked all four push options):** "second warm accent" delivered as the elevated amber, NOT a banned second ochre (cream+ochre+oxblood is a foundry slop reflex sitting right next to our burgundy). "Rethink the direction" read as "evolve boldly, keep the ledger DNA" — the ledger IS the wedge, so it stays; the two signature elements (line item + sun patch) are unchanged. This ENRICHES the Open Book system, it does not replace it.
- **Verified in-browser:** AA all pass (on-deep/burgundy 9.66, amber/burgundy 5.23, muted/blush 4.77); build exit 0 / 26 pages; 0 overflow at 1440/390/375; no-JS complete. Tokens source of truth = `tokens.css` (Committed-color block); design authority note = `open-book-handoff.md` §0.J.
- **User preferences learned:** on a brand/marketing surface Ryan wants committed, drenched color and visible life, not tasteful restraint; "static/plain" is his recurring failure signal (4th touch); the fix he responds to is color commitment (a deep band + a warm second accent + section rhythm), verified before presenting.
